加密的必要性和目的

Necessity and Purpose of Encryption

1. 数据传输时加密的必要性

  • 📶
    网络传输安全 (Network Transmission Security)

    数据在传输过程中容易被截取和窃听

    Data is vulnerable to interception and eavesdropping during transmission

  • 🔒
    隐私保护 (Privacy Protection)

    防止敏感信息（如密码、银行账号）泄漏

    Prevents sensitive information (passwords, bank accounts) from being exposed

  • 🛡️
    恶意攻击防护 (Malicious Attack Prevention)

    防止黑客和攻击者获取和篡改数据

    Prevents hackers and attackers from accessing and tampering with data

2. 加密的主要目的

  • 🔄
    数据转换 (Data Transformation)

    将数据转换为不可读形式，只有授权用户可以解密

    Transform data into unreadable form, only authorized users can decrypt

  • 💯
    保证数据完整性 (Data Integrity Assurance)

    确保数据在传输过程中未被篡改或损坏

    Ensure data remains unaltered and uncorrupted during transmission

  • ✅
    身份验证 (Identity Verification)

    确认数据发送者和接收者的身份

    Verify the identity of both sender and receiver

3. 数据安全的重要性

  • 🏦
    金融交易安全 (Financial Transaction Security)

    保护网上银行、电子支付等金融业务

    Protect online banking, electronic payments, and financial services

  • 🏢
    企业数据保护 (Corporate Data Protection)

    保护商业机密、客户信息等敏感数据

    Protect trade secrets, customer information, and sensitive corporate data

  • 📱
    个人信息安全 (Personal Information Security)

    保护个人隐私、通信记录等个人数据

    Protect personal privacy, communication records, and individual data
